🎬 Movie Club

Members only 🍿

This is a private movie club. Enter the club passcode to join. You'll only need to do this once on each device.

How it works

New here? Start with this
  1. Suggest films. Add anything you'd like the club to watch to the Film bank using Suggest a film. There's no limit. A link or a one-line pitch helps sell it.
  2. Star what you'd watch. Browse the bank and tap ☆ on films you'd happily watch. Stars carry over between rounds, and the most-starred films get onto the ballot.
  3. Vote on the ballot. Each round has a ballot of 6 films, picked automatically: the 4 most-starred, plus the ones that have waited longest. You get 3 votes. Tap a film's circle to vote, and tap again to take it back. You can change your votes until voting closes.
  4. Movie night. Whoever's organising opens Set or change movie night and adds the date and place. Voting closes 24 hours before. The film with the most votes wins, and ties go to the film that has waited longest.
  5. After the night. Someone presses We watched it ✓. The film moves to Already watched and a fresh ballot starts.

Good to know

  • Everyone's already seen a ballot film? Use swap out to replace it with another from the bank. It stays in the bank.
  • Seen a bank film outside the club? Use mark watched on it.
  • Night called off? Use Cancel this night in the movie night panel. Votes are kept, and voting stays open until a new night is set.
  • Want to watch a particular film? When setting up the night, choose it under Film instead of "Decided by vote". It needs to be in the bank first. The ballot and everyone's votes carry over to the next night.
  • No night set yet? Voting stays open and the top of the page shows which film is leading.
  • Your name appears on your suggestions and votes. You can change it at the top right.
  • New phone or cleared your browser? You'll need the club passcode again. Your votes stay with the old device for the current round.
  • Made a mistake? You can remove films you suggested, and back to bank undoes a "watched".
